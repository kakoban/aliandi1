05 / GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the calculator issue buy and sell signals?

No. It is a risk management and position sizing tool that models scenarios based on your inputs. Signals are shared in our Telegram channel.

What mathematical formulas are used?

For linear USDT contracts, R is risk budget, E entry, S stop, T target, and f fee per side:

R = balance × risk% / 100 f = fee% / 100 q = R / (|E − S| + f × (E + S)) Notional = q × E Stop loss incl. fees = q × (|E − S| + f × (E + S)) Net P/L = q × direction × (T − E) − q × f × (E + T) Initial margin = Notional / Leverage Liquidation (long) ≈ E × (1 − 1/L) / (1 − m) Liquidation (short) ≈ E × (1 + 1/L) / (1 + m)
How are live prices fetched from Binance?

Clicking the fetch button queries the official Binance Futures REST endpoint and populates the entry price field with a green/red flash animation.
